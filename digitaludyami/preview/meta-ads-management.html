❓ Questions

Meta Ads, answered

Can Meta ads really bring more customers?

Yes, for most businesses that sell to consumers or local customers. Facebook and Instagram let you reach people by city, age and interests, and start conversations on WhatsApp. Results depend on your offer and follow-up, which is why we start with a free audit.

Is the ad budget separate from your charges?

Yes. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta from your own card, so you always see exactly what was spent. Our charges depend on your goals and budget, and we share a clear quote after the free audit.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your city, product and how much a customer is worth to you. After the audit we recommend a starting budget that’s enough to test properly, and increase it only once ads are working.

How soon will I see enquiries?

Meta ads can bring enquiries within days of launch. The first few weeks are for testing, so results usually get better and more consistent over the first month or two.

Who owns the ad account and Pixel?

You do. The ad account, Pixel, audiences and Facebook page stay in your name. We work on them with partner access, which you control.

Do you make the ads and reels?

Yes. Ad creative, reels and copy are included. We test several versions and replace tired ads before results drop.

Can you guarantee a number of leads?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. What you get from us is a sound setup, honest testing, clear reporting and steady improvement.
