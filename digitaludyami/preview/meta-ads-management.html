❓ Questions

Meta Ads, answered

Is the ad budget separate from your charges?

Yes. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta from your own card, so you always see exactly what was spent. Our management charges depend on your goals and budget, and we share a clear quote after the free audit.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your city, product and how much a customer is worth to you. After the free audit we recommend a starting budget that’s enough to test properly, and we only suggest increasing it once ads are working.

How soon will I see enquiries?

Meta Ads can bring enquiries within days of launch. The first few weeks are about testing, so results usually get better and more consistent over the first month or two.

Who owns the ad account and Pixel?

You do. The ad account, Pixel, audiences and Facebook page stay in your name. We work on them with partner access, which you control.

Do you make the ad creatives and reels?

Yes. Ad creative, reels and copy are included. We test several versions and replace tired ads before results drop.

Lead forms or WhatsApp ads: which is better?

Both work for different businesses. Lead forms are quick to fill but need a qualifying question to keep quality up. WhatsApp click ads start a real conversation. We often test both and keep what brings better customers.

Can you guarantee a number of leads or sales?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. Results depend on your offer, market and follow-up. What you get from us is a sound setup, honest testing, clear reporting and steady improvement.
